# Cookbook  

## Fried Chicken  
**Ingredients (Serves 4):**  
- 8 chicken drumsticks or thighs  
- 2 cups all-purpose flour  
- 2 tsp salt  
- 1 tsp black pepper  
- 1 tsp paprika  
- 1 tsp garlic powder  
- 1 tsp onion powder  
- 1 cup buttermilk  
- Vegetable oil for frying  

**Steps:**  
1. Marinate chicken in buttermilk for at least 30 minutes (overnight is best).  
2. Mix flour and seasonings in a large bowl.  
3. Heat oil in a deep skillet or Dutch oven to **350°F (175°C)**.  
4. Dredge each piece of chicken in the seasoned flour, shaking off excess.  
5. Fry chicken in batches for **12–15 minutes**, turning occasionally, until golden brown and internal temp reaches **165°F (74°C)**.  
6. Drain on paper towels and serve hot.

## Mac and Cheese  
**Ingredients (Serves 6):**  
- 1 lb elbow macaroni  
- 4 cups shredded cheddar cheese (or mix of cheddar & mozzarella)  
- 3 cups milk  
- 1/4 cup butter  
- 1/4 cup flour  
- 1/2 tsp salt  
- 1/2 tsp black pepper  
- 1/2 tsp mustard powder (optional)  

**Steps:**  
1. Cook macaroni according to package directions until al dente. Drain and set aside.  
2. In a saucepan, melt butter over medium heat. Stir in flour and cook for **1–2 minutes** to make a roux.  
3. Slowly whisk in milk, stirring constantly until thickened (**about 5 minutes**).  
4. Add cheese, salt, pepper, and mustard powder. Stir until smooth.  
5. Combine pasta and sauce in a baking dish.  
6. Bake at **350°F (175°C)** for **20–25 minutes** for a golden, bubbly top.

## Green Beans  
**Ingredients (Serves 4):**  
- 1 lb fresh green beans, trimmed (or 2 cans cut green beans)  
- 2 tbsp butter or olive oil  
- 1 clove garlic, minced (optional)  
- Salt & pepper to taste  

**Steps:**  
1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil. Add green beans and cook for **4–5 minutes** until tender-crisp.  
2. Drain and immediately rinse under cold water to stop cooking.  
3. In a skillet, heat butter or oil over medium heat. Add garlic (if using) and cook for **1 minute**.  
4. Add green beans, season with salt and pepper, and sauté for **2–3 minutes**.  
5. Serve warm.

## Dinner Rolls  
**Ingredients (Serves 8):**  
- 1 package store-bought dinner roll dough (e.g., Rhodes rolls) OR homemade dough  
- 2 tbsp melted butter  

**Steps (for frozen dough rolls):**  
1. Place frozen rolls in a greased baking pan, spaced slightly apart.  
2. Cover with a clean towel and let rise in a warm place for **3–5 hours** until doubled in size.  
3. Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**.  
4. Bake rolls for **15–20 minutes**, until golden brown.  
5. Brush with melted butter before serving.
